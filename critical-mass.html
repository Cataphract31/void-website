How it works
There is no multiplier. Every tick the reactor takes some of the people inside, and their SOL is split instantly between everyone still standing. That is the only way anyone earns. Walk out whenever you like and keep what you're holding. If you're the last one inside, the reactor is yours.

Entry is fixed at 0.1 SOL so nobody can be a bigger slice of the pot than anyone else. The door takes 4% for the house and 3% for the Bonanza, which is paid back to players — 96% RTP.
