Critical Mass

How to play
Feed
House

Everyone pays 0.1 ◎ and steps into the reactor. Every tick it takes some of the people inside, and their SOL is split instantly between everyone still standing. That is the only way anyone earns.

Cashing out

Walk out whenever you like and keep exactly what you're holding. Last one inside takes the whole reactor.

Heat

A packed reactor is hungry — around 7.5% a tick. As people die and leave it cools toward 0.4%. Staying gets safer the longer you last, but there's nobody left to pay you. Safe and poor, or crowded and rich.

Grace

Nobody can be taken in the first two ticks. Look before it bites.

Bonanza

Every tick inside earns tickets, faster while the reactor is hot. They lock in when you walk out and carry between rounds. The jackpot fires about 1 round in 1500 however big it has grown — one holder takes everything and all tickets void, like a lottery draw.

The numbers

The door takes 7%: 4% house, 3% Bonanza. The Bonanza returns to players, so long-run return is 96%. Every payout is money other players brought — no bankroll, nothing to drain.
