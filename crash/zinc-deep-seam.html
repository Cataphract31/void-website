Deep Seam

How it works
Feed
House

Everyone pays 0.1 ◎ and climbs down into the seam. The more bodies packed in the shaft, the faster the air goes bad — carbon dioxide pools in the bottom and the oxygen runs out.

How you earn

Every tick the gas takes some of the miners down there. Everything they were carrying is split instantly between everyone still digging. That is the only way anyone earns — there is no clock bonus, no multiplier, only other people's ore.

Climbing out

Haul up whenever you like and keep exactly what you're carrying. If you're the last one down there, the whole seam is yours.

The air

A packed shaft is deadly — about 7.5% a tick. As miners die or climb out there are fewer lungs down there, the gas recedes, and it drops toward 0.4%. Staying gets safer the longer you last — but there's nobody left to pay you. Safe and poor, or crowded and rich.

Fresh air

Nobody can be taken in the first two ticks. Look before it turns.

The Bonanza vein

Every tick underground earns tickets, faster while the air is worst. They lock in when you climb out and carry between rounds. The vein is struck about 1 round in 1500 however rich it has grown — one holder takes everything and all tickets void, like a lottery draw.

The numbers

The door takes 7%: 4% house, 3% to the vein. The vein pays back to players, so long-run return is 96%. Every payout is ore other miners brought down — no bankroll, nothing to drain.
